# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 17, 2026  
**Platform Name:** Humid1 (humid1.com)  

This Privacy Policy explains how Humid1 ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website, IoT platform, and support channels.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate our platform, secure your account, and support your connected hardware:

* **Account Information:** When you register an account, we collect your name, username, email address, and account credentials.
* **Device Telemetry:** If you connect IoT hardware to our service, we collect and store the telemetry data, sensor readings, and configuration states transmitted by your devices.
* **Communications:** When you use our support or chat tools, we collect the contents of your messages and any information you provide when requesting help.
* **Technical Data:** We automatically collect standard server logs, IP addresses, and browser details for security, rate-limiting, and debugging purposes.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to:

* Provide, maintain, and secure your account and connected devices.
* Process device telemetry and display your dashboard data.
* Respond to your support requests and communicate service updates.
* Enforce our Acceptable Use Policy and protect against malicious activity, security breaches, or unapproved device access.

### 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

* **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or device telemetry to third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.
* **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of our users and platform.

### 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures - such as encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), firewall restrictions, and secure credential storage - to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 5. Data Retention

We retain your account information, chat logs, and telemetry data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. You may request account deletion and data removal by contacting us at the address below.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the platform after updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
