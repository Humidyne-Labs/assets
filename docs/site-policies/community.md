# Humid1 Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

Welcome to the Humid1 community! This space is designed for makers, hobbyists, and users to help each other troubleshoot hardware builds, share configurations, and discuss IoT humidor management.

---

### 1. Purpose & Scope

This chat is a peer-to-peer self-help space focused on **Humid1 platform usage, ThingsBoard integration, ESP32 microcontrollers, and DIY IoT hardware builds**. While official support is limited, our community thrives on shared knowledge and collaboration.

### 2. Respect & Civility

* **Be Respectful:** Treat fellow makers and administrators with courtesy and respect. Disagreements happen, but personal attacks, harassment, bullying, or toxic behavior will not be tolerated.
* **Inclusive Environment:** Hate speech, slurs, or discriminatory language based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or background will result in an immediate and permanent ban.

### 3. Stay on Topic

* Keep discussions relevant to IoT, hardware, sensors, firmware, and humidor management.
* Avoid derailing troubleshooting threads with unrelated political, religious, or off-topic debates.

### 4. No Spam, Solicitation, or Advertising

* Do not use the chat to spam links, promote commercial products, pitch unrelated services, or run unauthorized self-promotion.
* Sharing open-source repositories, helpful DIY tutorials, or personal project links is encouraged as long as it directly aids the community.

### 5. Protect Your Privacy & Security

* **Never share sensitive data** in public chat channels, including account passwords, API tokens, private keys, Wi-Fi credentials, or personal identification information.
* If you need to share code snippets or configuration files for debugging, make sure to scrub any hardcoded secrets or credentials first.

### 6. Moderation & Enforcement

* Chat moderators and platform administrators reserve the right to edit, remove, or delete any messages that violate these guidelines.
* Users who repeatedly violate these rules or disrupt the community will have their chat privileges revoked and may face account suspension on Humid1.
